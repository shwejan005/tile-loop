# TileLoop — demo guide

For a timed five-minute evaluator presentation with exact source files to open, use [DEMO_SCRIPT.md](./DEMO_SCRIPT.md).

Start at <http://127.0.0.1:3000> and point out the **B2B-first** positioning: tile installers and contractors are the primary users; homeowners and clients are read-only viewers. Pricing and team-seat needs are not validated. Then open <http://127.0.0.1:3000/demo>. The sample is interactive and does not require sign-in.

1. Show the L-shaped room preview, obstacle, floor opening, and doorway marker.
2. Open **Room geometry**, choose custom under **Room outline**, upload a PNG/JPEG/WebP plan, click **Trace room outline**, add boundary points, then apply. The image stays in the browser; only the measured outline is saved.
3. Try one-third offset, then herringbone (requires the demo’s 2:1 tile format). Compare centered and top-left origins.
4. Switch between **2D** and **3D**. Select a piece to show its ID and dimensions.
5. Review the cut schedule, heuristic offcut candidates, and carton estimate.
6. Point out that the sample carton size is illustrative. The layout estimate is heuristic and needs installer review.

The public demo does not save. To save plans or record actual installation outcomes, create an account and open a project.
